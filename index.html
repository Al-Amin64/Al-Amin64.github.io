About Me

WHO I AM

The person behind the commits.

Hey — I'm Md. Al-Amin  , a 17-year-old passionate coder and Cadet currently studying in the 11th grade at Faujdarhat Cadet College, Bangladesh.

My journey into the world of coding began as a hobby back in the 6th grade, and since then I've been passionately exploring this diverse field. From game development to web development, robotics, and competitive programming — I've delved into various areas of technology. Each experience broadened my understanding and fueled my curiosity.

Recently I've been trying to learn machine learning and found it extremely fascinating and full of potential. The idea that systems can learn from data and make intelligent decisions feels borderline magical — and I'm here for it.

Living a disciplined cadet life simultaneously means building under real constraints — time pressure, limited resources, actual deadlines. It makes me a sharper engineer. Precision and persistence are my defaults. Feel free to connect if you share similar interests or want to collaborate!

Philosophy

HOW I THINK

"Each domain of code is a universe with its own rules — mastering one teaches you how to learn the next."
— The Cadet's Approach

I think of every skill I pick up as its own separate universe — web, games, networking, vision, ML. Each has distinct tools, distinct failure modes, and its own kind of satisfaction when something clicks.

The discipline of cadet life feeds directly into this: building under real constraints, shipping despite imperfect conditions, and iterating relentlessly. I care less about polished outcomes and more about whether a project asks a real question: What if? How else? Can this be simpler?

This portfolio is a snapshot of those questions — in motion, incomplete, and still growing.
